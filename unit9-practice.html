Task 2: Practice introducing yourself and talking about your skills.

Luyện tập giới thiệu bản thân và nói về các kỹ năng của bạn (Tự nói).

Gợi ý

1. Introduction: Good morning/afternoon. My name is... I recently graduated with a degree in...

2. Strengths: I am very good at... I also work well under pressure and enjoy...

3. Experience: At university/In my last job, I worked on team projects and I enjoy sharing ideas.

4. Goal: I want to learn and grow here, especially in the field of...
