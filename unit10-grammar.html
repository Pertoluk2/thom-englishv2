Task 2: Complete the sentences with must, have to, must not, or don't have to (Obligations).

Hoàn thành các câu sau bằng cách sử dụng **must, have to, must not** hoặc **don't have to**.

  1. You take your medicine twice a day, or your symptoms won't improve.
  2. You drink cold water; it could make your sore throat worse.
  3. You go to the clinic every day. Once a week is enough.
  4. Patients wear a mask in the hospital at all times.
  5. You eat before taking these pills, or they might upset your stomach.
