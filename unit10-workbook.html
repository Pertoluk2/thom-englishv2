DAY 3: Medical Advice & Symptoms

  • - I have to take my medication twice a day.
  • - I must eat before taking the pills.
  • - I mustn't go back to work for 14 days.
  • - I don't have to do any special exercises. A gentle daily walk is fine.
  • - I have to call Dr. Turner immediately if I feel sick again.

  3. Can she go back work immediately?
  4. Does she have to do any special exercise?
