Task 2: Choose the correct information based on an online profile (Day 3).

Dựa trên hồ sơ của Hamid Syal, trả lời các câu hỏi sau:

HAMID SYAL: Creative and proactive marketing professional with varied experience in the travel industry. Started as a receptionist in the hotel industry, worked up to deputy manager. Worked in Japan, India, South Africa, for prestigious hotels (The Ritz). Advises travel agencies, gives presentations at industry events. **Skills**: Excellent people skills, enjoys working in teams. **Qualifications**: BS Business and Hospitality Management, London (2010); Diploma in Marketing, CIM (2015).

  1. What industry does Hamid work in? Hotels Travel Airlines
  2. Where has Hamid worked before? A department store A restaurant A hotel
  3. How does Hamid describe his people skills? Average Good Excellent
  4. In what situation does Hamid say he enjoys working? Alone In teams With clients
