C. Practice

Task 1 & 2: Listen and fill in the blanks

Nghe đoạn hội thoại và điền từ còn thiếu vào chỗ trống. Sau đó, trả lời các câu hỏi.

Martin: Pilar. There's something wrong with this (1) . I keep pressing the button but it won't do anything.

Pilar: Oh dear. Do you want me to give you a (2) , Martin?

Martin: Thanks.

Pilar: Have you any idea what this (3) means? The one flashing here.

Martin: Maybe it means there is some (4) stuck inside. It might be worth (5) it and having a look.

Pilar: How do I do that?

Martin: You could try (6) that lever there. That's the one.

Pilar: Oh yes. You're right. There is some paper (7) in here. Thanks a lot. I think that's sorted it now.

Task 3: Put the sentences into the correct order

Sắp xếp các câu để tạo thành một đoạn hội thoại đúng. Kéo các câu vào đúng vị trí.

Sophia: The printer is by the meeting rooms, near the back.
Sophia: They're in the same drawer as the stapler, right next to the pens.
Tom: Hey, do you know where the stapler is?
Tom: Thanks! And where's the printer?
Tom: Perfect, thanks for your help!
Sophia: It's in the supply drawer near the kitchen.
Tom: Got it! And do you know where the paper clips are?

Task 4: Complete the conversation (Speaking)

Hoàn thành đoạn hội thoại dựa trên các gợi ý. Bạn có thể luyện nói đoạn hội thoại này thật to.

Emily: **[Hỏi xem chiếc dập ghim ở đâu]**

Ryan: It's in the supply drawer.

Emily: **[Hỏi xem kẹp giấy ở đâu]**

Ryan: They're in the same drawer as the stapler, right next to the pens.

Emily: **[Cảm ơn và hỏi cách in tài liệu]**

Ryan: Just go to the printer and send your document from the computer.

Emily: **[Hỏi xem máy in ở đâu]**

Ryan: It's down the hall, near the copy room.

Emily: **[Cảm ơn và hỏi cách sử dụng máy photocopy]**

Ryan: It's easy. Just place your document on the scanner, press 'Copy,' and then select how many copies you want.

Emily: **[Cảm ơn]**

Ryan: You're welcome!
