Task 2: Fill in the gap using "Do" or "Does"

Chọn từ đúng để hoàn thành mỗi câu hỏi.

  1. Do Does the come in early?
  2. Do Does he have a key for this drawer?
  3. Do Does your laptop have a DVD drive?
  4. Do Does Jim and Tom have new screens?
  5. Do Does you keep pens in your desk drawer?
  6. Do Does Sarah write the minutes?
  7. Do Does all employees have wall calendars?

Task 3: Cross out the incorrect word in each question

Nhấp vào từ đúng trong mỗi câu hỏi.

  1. When / What are you going on vacation?
  2. Where / How are the cups?
  3. Who / What is the photocopier code?
  4. Why / How do I turn off the screen?
  5. Why / Who is this drawer always locked?
  6. Where / When does the cafeteria open?
  7. Why / Who do I ask for printer ink?
  8. What / When do you discuss at meetings?
