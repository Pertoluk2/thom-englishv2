Bài tập 2: Đọc và trả lời câu hỏi

Lees het gesprek en beantwoord de meerkeuzevragen.

Colleague 1: Hey, do you need any supplies for the office? I noticed there's a sale at the office supply store.

Colleague 2: That's great! What's on sale?

Colleague 1: Well, the printer paper is on sale for $5.99 per pack. Usually, it's $9.50, so it's a pretty good deal.

Colleague 2: That sounds like a good deal. How about pens? We always run out of pens.

Colleague 1: Pens are $2.99 for a pack of 10. They're usually $4.50, so it's a good price too. I'll grab a couple of packs.

Colleague 2: I need a new desk chair. Do you know how much they are?

Colleague 1: They have desk chairs starting at $79.99, but the one I want is $129. It's ergonomic and has good reviews.

Colleague 2: I'll have to check that out. By the way, I'm planning to grab lunch. Have you tried the new café nearby?

Colleague 1: Yes, I've been there. They have a lunch special for $12.99: a sandwich, soup, and a drink. It's a pretty good deal for a quick lunch.

Colleague 2: That sounds delicious. I might join you.
