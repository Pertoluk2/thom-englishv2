Silvia's Blog: On the up!

I've just started my new job at Moda Fashions in Edinburgh. I've worked for three different employers so far, and I'm hoping that this job will be the best I've had. My first job was in a local supermarket. I hated it. I was bored, I had no responsibilities, and the customers were often rude to me. I left after only three months. After that, I worked in a bar. It was more interesting and I met some interesting people. I was studying for my business diploma while I was working there, so I often had to stay up late to study. The money was okay, but I was always tired. I was working as an intern until last month. The salary was very low, but I learned a lot. And now I've started my new job as an assistant in the Sales department. I'm sure it will be a great experience.
